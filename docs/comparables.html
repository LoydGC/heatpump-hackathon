Comparable products

Compiled 2026-11-07. Products similar to a guided heat pump sizing tool with cost and CO2 results. Descriptive only; nothing here recommends an approach. Utility/government sizing tools are in the earlier research file and only summarised here.

Link status: ✓ opened and read · ✗ blocked or failed · ~ taken from search or another page, not opened. "–" means not found on the pages we could read.

Top 5 most relevant

  1. Rewiring America API: Residential Electrification Model: Address plus heating fuel in; fuel use, rates and emissions factors out. Closest to 'location in, cost and CO2 out'.
  2. HEAT (Code for Boston): Open source heat-load-vs-outdoor-temperature charting and heat pump capacity check. Same core idea as sizing.
  3. NEEA dual-fuel control analysis: Switchover temperature vs gas use, cost and CO2 with worked numbers.
  4. Mass Save Switchover Temperature Calculator: Only consumer tool found that frames switchover as cost-optimal vs emissions-oriented.
  5. Palmetto Energy Intelligence API: Infers 60+ home traits with user override; hourly load, cost and carbon. 500 free calls/month.

Palmetto Energy Intelligence API

CostCO2Infers home
Runs it
Palmetto (clean-energy company)
Inputs
'Any home in the U.S.'; model infers 60+ building characteristics and each can be overridden; calibrates to actual usage if supplied. Whether the key is an address is not stated on the page.
Outputs
Hourly load by end use and fuel, baseline plus upgrade scenarios (60+ upgrades incl. HVAC), utility cost, savings, carbon footprint. Heat pump sizing not mentioned. Free to 500 calls/month, then reported at $0.05/call.

Heat load & sizing

HEAT: Home Energy Analysis Tool (Code for Boston / HeatSmart Alliance)

Sizing
Runs it
Code for Boston volunteers with HeatSmart Alliance; open source (MIT) https://github.com/codeforboston/home-energy-analysis-tool
Inputs
Home location, thermostat settings, historical gas bills (National Grid / Eversource), basic home info; temperature from a nearby weather station. Free account required.
Outputs
Heat loss rate and design heating load; chart of heating requirement vs outdoor temperature; comparison with other homes by floor area; charts to judge whether a proposed heat pump's capacity fits.
UX notes
Closest open-source analogue: heat-load-vs-outdoor-temperature chart is the core visual. Stack per README: React/Remix, Python via Pyodide in the browser. Built for energy coaches.
Gap
Needs a year of bills (US gas only); no cost, CO2 or backup-fuel results; Massachusetts utilities.

Dual-fuel / balance point

Mass Save Switchover Temperature Calculator

CostBackup / dual-fuelCO2
Runs it
Mass Save (MA utilities); hosted externally (app.efficiencyestimator.com)
Inputs
Not visible (hosted tool not opened).
Outputs
Finds the 'most optimal, cost-effective switchover temperature' from current fuel prices. Page notes the natural-gas maximum of 30°F is 'designed to support decreased greenhouse gas emissions rather than operational savings', and that customers can set the switchover to maximize heat pump use, minimize cost, or meet other goals.
UX notes
Explicitly frames switchover as a choice between lowest cost and lowest emissions.
Gap
Inputs/outputs of the embedded tool unverified; no home-specific sizing.
Link status
✓ page / ✗ tool

NEEA: Dual-Fuel Furnace + Heat Pump Control Analysis (Larson Energy Research, Mar 2026) and VCHP Levelized Cost Tool

SizingCostBackup / dual-fuelCO2
Runs it
NEEA (utility-funded alliance) / Larson Energy Research
Inputs
Spreadsheet tool (originally CEE) runs hourly 8760 calc from TMY weather files.
Outputs
Slides define balance point (capacity = load) vs switchover; switchover is 'the largest lever' on gas use (Portland example: 309 therms/yr at 40°F, 154 at 35°F, 105 at 30°F). Recommends ~30°F balance point and switchover a few degrees above. Portland example: furnace+AC $1,093 / 8,127 lb CO2 vs single-speed HP with 30°F switchover $821 / 4,699 lb.
UX notes
Best source of cost-and-CO2 numbers for hybrid operation; shows results as a function of switchover temperature.
Gap
A spreadsheet and slides, not a consumer product. Pacific NW climate and prices.
Link status
✓ (slides) / tool itself not opened

Gaps across the field

  • No tool found that combines all of: location-driven climate, heat pump capacity vs load, supplemental/backup heat, and cost plus CO2 results.
  • Dual-fuel switchover appears as a standalone calculator (Mass Save) or analyst spreadsheet (NEEA/CEE), not inside a guided home flow.
  • Address-based inference exists (Rewiring America, Palmetto) but the docs we could read don't show capacity sizing or backup heat.
  • Sizing tools that need utility bills (HEAT) or a full Manual J (PNNL, NRCan) are aimed at coaches/contractors.
  • Nothing found for Fairbanks or Montreal specifically; Alaska tools are in earlier research.

Not found or not checkable

  • Mitsubishi, Daikin, Carrier, Trane: no homeowner-facing sizing tool found; their selection software appears to be contractor-only. A Daikin white paper (BPG-EXTAMB, ~ https://www.DaikinAC.com/content/assets/DOC/White-papers-/BPG-EXTAMB.pdf) turned up in search but was not opened.
  • Cleanbit: https://www.cleanbit.ca/ blocked automated access (robots), so nothing could be confirmed.
  • Heatpumps.ca: no dedicated NRCan 'heatpumps.ca' product found; the Canadian entries here are NRCan's toolkit (earlier research) and the Canadian Climate Institute calculator.
  • Many 'heat pump calculator' sites on *.pages.dev and similar are SEO pages and were excluded.
