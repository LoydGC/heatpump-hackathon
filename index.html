What's a heat pump?

A heat pump moves heat instead of making it. Even freezing air holds heat. The outdoor unit gathers it and pumps it inside, the way a fridge moves heat out of its box, just in reverse.

Because it moves heat rather than burning fuel, a heat pump can be two to three times as efficient as ordinary electric heat. In summer it runs backwards and cools your home too.

About rebates

Rebates are one-time discounts or tax credits that lower what you pay for a heat pump. They come from several places, and what's available depends on where you live and what you heat with today.
