Placeholder assumptions

Temperatures are synthesized from 1991–2020 monthly normals with an assumed spread, not real hourly data. Heat load is heat loss × (balance point − outdoor temp). Heat pump size is its capacity at 5°F; capacity falls in the cold and stops below the cutoff, and the current heat covers the rest. Efficiency (COP) runs from about 4 at 47°F to 1.4 at −15°F. Wood is counted as zero CO₂ by default. Replace all of it with the team's data.
